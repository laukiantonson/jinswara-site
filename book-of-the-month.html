BOOK OF THE MONTH — AUGUST 2026

सत्य की खोज

by Dr. Hukamchand Bharill · Pandit Todarmal Smarak Trust, Jaipur

About the Book

A search for the soul, told as a story.

Summary

Tattvagyan in story form

सत्य की खोज is a novel — a seeker’s journey towards truth, the soul, and bhed-vigyan, written in simple everyday language. Deep Jain philosophy unfolds through characters and conversations, making it the ideal first book for anyone beginning their Swadhyay journey.

Details

267 pages

Novel · Dravyanuyog / Tattvagyan · Paperback & PDF

Languages

Hindi · Gujarati

Printed editions available via the JinWani portal.

— सत्य की खोज
